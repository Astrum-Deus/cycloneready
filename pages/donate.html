Helping communities prepare for and recover from cyclones starts with people coming together. Explore ways to support organisations working in Myanmar.

Why Support Disaster Relief?

Cyclones can damage homes, disrupt essential services and leave communities without the supplies they need. Donations, useful supplies and volunteering can help support disaster response and recovery efforts. Even a small contribution can make a difference when people work together.

MORE THAN MONEY

Other Ways to Help

Financial donations are not the only way to contribute. You can also support communities through practical actions.

Donate Supplies

Consider contributing useful essentials such as clean water, non-perishable food, hygiene products and first-aid supplies through trusted organisations.

Volunteer

Offer your time and skills to recognised community organisations. Check their requirements and safety instructions before volunteering.

Spread Awareness

Share reliable preparedness information and official emergency updates. Avoid spreading unverified warnings or misleading information.

Give Responsibly

Before donating, make sure that the organisation and donation method are legitimate. Use official websites and verified contact information, and check what supplies are actually needed before sending them.
