How Human Activities Affect Cyclones

Our actions can change the environment and make some cyclone impacts worse. Discover how human activities affect our planet and the communities living in it.

Explore the Impacts

UNDERSTANDING THE CONNECTION

Cyclones are natural, but human actions matter.

Cyclones form naturally over warm ocean water. However, human activities such as burning fossil fuels, cutting down trees and throwing rubbish into drains can affect the environment. These actions can increase some cyclone-related dangers, such as heavy rainfall, coastal damage and flooding.

Did You Know?

Human activities do not directly create cyclones. but climate change can increase rainfall rates in tropical cyclones and raise the potential for stronger storms. This does not mean every cyclone becomes stronger or that there will always be more cyclones.

THREE HUMAN ACTIVITIES

How We Affect Cyclone Impacts

1.

Burning Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil and gas releases greenhouse gases into the air. These gases trap heat and warm the Earth. A warmer atmosphere can hold more water, which can lead to heavier rainfall during cyclones.

Can increase cyclone rainfall

2.

Cutting Down Trees

Cutting down trees and clearing forests can damage the natural environment. When mangroves and coastal trees are removed, communities may lose some natural protection from waves, storm surges and coastal erosion.

Less natural coastal protection

3.

Throwing Trash into Drains

When people throw rubbish into drains, the waste can block the flow of water. During heavy rain, water cannot drain away easily and may build up on streets, causing worse flooding in nearby areas.

Increased risk of street flooding

Why Mangroves Are Important

Mangroves are trees that grow along tropical coastlines. Their strong roots help hold the soil together and reduce coastal erosion. They can also slow waves and help protect nearby communities from some storm impacts.

Protecting mangrove forests is an important way to care for our coastal environment.

MAKE A DIFFERENCE

What Can We Do?

Protect Nature

Protect trees, plant suitable vegetation and help care for mangrove forests.

Reduce Waste

Throw rubbish into bins and keep drains and waterways clean.

Save Energy

Save electricity, avoid wasting energy and support cleaner energy choices.

These small actions can change alot

Taking care of the environment can help reduce some disaster risks. By protecting nature, reducing waste and preparing for severe weather, we can help make our communities safer.
