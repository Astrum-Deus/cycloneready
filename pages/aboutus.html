OUR PURPOSE

Why We Exist

Why CycloneReady?

Cyclones can have serious effects on communities across Myanmar, especially in coastal and low-lying areas. However, knowing the risks and preparing early can help protect lives.

We created CycloneReady to make cyclone information easier to understand and accessible to everyone. We hope our website encourages people to take warnings seriously, prepare for emergencies, and stay safe.

"Knowledge and preparation can make a difference."

WHAT WE DID

How We Contributed

Three ways we worked together to make CycloneReady.

We researched cyclone formation, historical cyclones in Myanmar, their effects, and ways people can reduce cyclone risks.
