EMERGENCY FIELD GUIDE

Your Emergency Kit: More Than Just Supplies

When a cyclone strikes, everyday services may become unavailable. Your emergency kit helps you and your family stay safe, healthy and prepared.

A well-prepared emergency kit
01 / THE BASICS

Water and Food

Clean drinking water and ready-to-eat food are essential during emergencies. Flooding can contaminate water sources, while damaged roads may make shops difficult to reach. Keep safe drinking water and non-perishable food for your household.

02 / HEALTH

Health and First Aid

A first-aid kit can help you treat minor injuries when medical services are difficult to reach. Keep essential medicines with your supplies, especially if someone in your family needs them regularly.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Light, Power and Information

Cyclones can cause power outages and interrupt communication. A torch, spare batteries, phone, power bank and battery-powered radio can help you see in the dark and receive important weather updates.

Keep clothing and hygiene supplies dry
04 / PERSONAL CARE

Clothing and Hygiene

Dry clothes and basic hygiene supplies help you stay comfortable and reduce the risk of illness. Pack extra clothing, soap, toothbrushes and other personal hygiene items in waterproof bags.

05 / PROTECTION

Cash and Important Documents

ATMs, banks and electronic payment services may become unavailable during a disaster. Keep some cash and copies of important documents in a waterproof bag so they remain protected.

REMINDER

Prepare Before It Happens.

Every household has different needs. Check your supplies regularly, replace expired items and make sure everyone in your family knows where the emergency kit is kept.
