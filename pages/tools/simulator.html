Understanding the Conditions

Explore why each environmental factor matters when tropical cyclones develop.

01

Warm Ocean Water

Warm ocean surfaces provide heat and moisture that can fuel tropical cyclone development.

02

Atmospheric Moisture

Moist air helps thunderstorms develop and maintain their activity.

03

Wind Shear

Strong changes in wind speed or direction with height can disrupt storm organization.

04

Atmospheric Instability

Instability encourages rising air, which contributes to thunderstorm formation.
