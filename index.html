UNDERSTANDING THE RISK

Why does this matter?

Cyclones are natural hazards, but their effects can be made worse when people are unprepared or do not have access to important information.

WHY IT MATTERS

One storm can change thousands of lives.

01

People can be vulnerable

Strong winds, heavy rainfall and storm surges can damage homes, destroy infrastructure and put lives at risk.

02

Preparation makes a difference

Knowing evacuation routes, preparing emergency supplies and following warnings can reduce the danger.

03

Information should be accessible

Communities need clear and understandable information so they can make safer decisions before, during and after a cyclone.

OUR PURPOSE

Knowledge is one of our strongest forms of protection.

CycloneReady was created to make disaster preparedness information easier to understand and access. We want to help people recognise cyclone risks, learn practical safety measures and make informed decisions when a disaster threatens their community.

More about our project

Being informed is being prepared.
